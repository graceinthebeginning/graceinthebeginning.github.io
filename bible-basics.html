Lesson 1: What Is the Bible?

Welcome to your first Bible lesson! You do not need to know everything about the Bible to begin. We can learn slowly, one passage at a time.

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."
— Psalm 119:105 (ESV)

1. What is the Bible?

The Bible is a collection of sacred writings that Christians read to learn about God, faith, love, forgiveness, and how to live. It tells the story of God's relationship with people and points Christians toward Jesus Christ.

2. The two main parts

Old Testament

The Old Testament contains accounts of creation, the history of God's people, poetry, wisdom, and messages from prophets. It also helps us understand the background to the life of Jesus.

New Testament

The New Testament tells us about Jesus' life, teachings, death, and resurrection. It also includes the beginnings of the Christian church and letters that teach Christians about faith and daily life.

3. How many books are there?

The Protestant Bible contains 66 books: 39 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament. Some other Christian traditions include additional books in their Old Testament.

4. Where should a beginner start?

A simple place to begin is the Gospel of Mark in the New Testament. It introduces Jesus and describes his ministry in a direct, easy-to-follow way.

Your reading for today

Read: Mark 1:1–15

As you read, think about these questions:

  • What do I learn about Jesus?
  • What stands out to me?
  • What would I like to understand better?

A gentle reminder

You do not have to understand every verse immediately. Read patiently, ask questions, and return to passages when you need to. Learning is a journey.
